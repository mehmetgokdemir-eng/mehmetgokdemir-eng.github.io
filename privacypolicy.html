Dishpedia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Introduction

Dishpedia ("we", "our", "the app") is a food encyclopedia application developed by Nihan Gokdemir. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected through our app. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Data We Collect

Dishpedia is designed to work 100% offline and does not require any user account or login. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

All recipe data, bookmarks, notebooks, and shopping lists are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

Advertising

Dishpedia uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded ads, and native ads). Google AdMob may collect certain device information and use advertising identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your consent preferences.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain consent before showing personalized ads, in compliance with GDPR.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

Google AdMob — for serving advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data for ad delivery and analytics purposes.

Children's Privacy

Dishpedia is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's content (recipes and food history) is suitable for all ages.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is minimal risk to your personal information. All user-generated content (bookmarks, notes, shopping lists) remains on your device.

Your Choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. You can also watch a rewarded ad in the app to enjoy an ad-free experience for a limited time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
